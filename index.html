My profile

Who you are, your stories, your numbers, what drives you, samples of how you actually write. Used for every application. Saves itself as you type, so there is nothing to remember and nothing lost on refresh. Stored in this browser only, never in this site's source.

Unlock your profile

An encrypted profile is stored with this site. Enter your passphrase to decrypt it in this browser. You do this once per device, then it is saved locally and loads on its own.

Encrypt this profile for the hosted site

Encrypts the profile above and downloads profile.enc.json. Commit that file to the repo and the hosted site will ask for this passphrase instead of a file. The passphrase never leaves this browser and is not recoverable, so store it in your password manager before you close this.

The passphrase is the only protection. Anyone can download the encrypted file, so it can be attacked offline with no rate limit. Use a long unique passphrase from your password manager. Do not reuse one.
